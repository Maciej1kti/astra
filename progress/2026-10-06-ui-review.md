# Visual review of the interface — 2026-10-06

The owner asked for a review of the whole UI, the component system and motion
against the goal of a restrained, well-made minimalism, and for a check of
hard-coded values. This is a review only; nothing in the application changed.

## Method

- Read `apps/web/src/styles/*`, every component `<style>` block,
  `lib/ui/motion.ts`, `lib/ui/motion-layers.ts` and the design guide.
- Searched all styles for values that bypass `tokens.css`.
- Rendered the release build at `48c697b` on a disposable synthetic host with
  ordinary pairing: all eight views, the card editor and its menus, schedule
  dialog, settings, registration, report, diagnostics and pairing, at 1440px
  and 390px in light and dark, plus 820px views. Dialog and scene entrances
  were sampled by pausing running animations at fixed times.

## Token discipline

No raw colour, font size, font weight, shadow or letter-spacing exists outside
`tokens.css`. What does bypass it:

- Component dimensions in pixels: `focus.css` (340/680/440/120/280/260),
  `editor.css` (336/560/408 and 3px range band), `CounterRow` (76/60/112/240),
  `CounterTrend` (112×26), chart components (128/150/152/180/240/280/420),
  `CalendarToolbar` (60/110), `Board` (320/520), `WorkspaceHeader` (4/8rem).
  About 35 comparable one-off dimensions are tokens, so the rule is uneven.
- Layers: `z-index: 5` in `CalendarView` (twice) and `GanttTask`, `2` in
  `dialog.css`.
- Opacity: `BoardCard` drag 0.35 beside `--drag-opacity` 0.28; 0.6 and 0.75 in
  charts.
- Motion: keyframe literals in `motion.css` (12px, -3px, 0.992, 0.96, 1.1,
  70ms, rotations), 100/220 ms popup delays in `CalendarView`, every layer
  delay and stagger in `motion.ts` and `motion-layers.ts`, and a 240 ms
  fallback in `layout-motion.ts` beside a 500 ms token.
- `--calendar-mobile-grid-height` (740px) is redefined inside `CalendarView`,
  so the token value is never used. `--wash` only aliases `--soft`.
- The dark palette is written out twice (explicit theme and system preference).
- Sixteen distinct breakpoints, including 700/720, 620/640 and 359/360 pairs.
- Icons mix the SVG set with text glyphs (↗ ← → ‹ › ✕ ↔ ↑) and native
  disclosure triangles; previous/next has three renderings.

## Visible defects

1. Board and Projects column titles are title-cased by `text-transform:
   capitalize`, giving "Do Sprawdzenia".
2. The focus ring of **Wybierz daty** is clipped by the schedule disclosure.
3. The card title field has no inline padding, so its hover and focus surface
   starts flush with the text and its ring reaches the dialog edge.
4. Compact Timeline row labels show three lines with the first one cut; the
   clamp has no hidden overflow.
5. On a phone the Focus section count wraps under the floating add button.
6. Settings: **Zmień użytkownika** and **Dodaj użytkownika** sit above the
   baseline of their fields.
7. A disabled primary button keeps its gradient and shadow at half opacity.
8. Date ranges render as `2026-09-07– 2026-09-09`, without the leading space.
9. The card-project chooser puts its label beside an unsized select in a
   600px dialog.

## Consistency

- Two card dialects: Focus pins use icons and relative dates; List, Board and
  Updates use a badge pile with ISO dates in the monospace face (`time` is
  monospace globally).
- Two board dialects: the project Board keeps the widget's flat bordered
  columns and 10px corners; Projects and the all-project Board use soft 20px
  panels.
- The type scale has eleven sizes, five of them between 12 and 16px; spacing
  has fifteen steps named by index.
- The primary button is the only glossy element; cancel is quiet in one dialog
  and bordered in another.

## Motion

A dialog is not readable before roughly 450 ms and finishes after 760 ms plus
section delays; a loaded view is blank at 200 ms and cascades for about 0.7 s
on every navigation, with up to six layers per card and blur on headings.
Hover feedback runs for 500 ms. Decorative responses (navigation icon scale,
pin and priority rotation, checkbox squeeze, row shift, arrow nudge, card
lift) add to that. Reduced motion is handled correctly throughout.

## Per-view observations

- **Shell:** `Owner` beside the settings icon and an ISO date in the header;
  on desktop **Więcej** repeats the sidebar; on a phone the add button and the
  dock both float over content.
- **Projects:** half of every card is a row of three quiet icons; the arrow
  means "move" here and "open" in Focus.
- **Board:** three ways to add a card at once; on a phone the status strip
  repeats the column header and the 52dvh board leaves empty space below.
- **Calendar:** two create buttons; the phone agenda wraps "Cały dzień" on
  every row and repeats the month on every day.
- **Timeline:** four rows of controls precede the chart (about 500px on a
  phone), including a selection panel with a disabled button.
- **List:** the project name repeats on every row of a project-scoped list;
  the empty state is a dashed box inside the table border.
- **Updates:** every kind shows the same bell.
- **Card editor:** good hierarchy; tag chips are 44px tall beside small tags
  elsewhere; a lone menu trigger floats under the counters; a new card shows
  every empty section, including the comment composer.

## Limits

Chromium only; emulated phone and tablet sizes, not physical devices. Motion
was judged from sampled frames, not measured for frame rate. Which findings to
act on, and any change to the documented motion vocabulary, is the owner's
decision.
