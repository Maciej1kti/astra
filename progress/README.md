# Implementation evidence

Use [current status](STATE.md), [scope decisions](SCOPE.md) and the
[code structure](../docs/CODE-STRUCTURE.md) for maintained guidance. Dated reports
and numbered evidence records describe their original revisions.

Historical logs, screenshots, one-off probes and compatibility test entry points
were retired from the working tree during the maintainability cleanup. Their
[complete published checkpoint](https://github.com/Maciej1kti/astra/tree/2a5530a8bb83eec0c3f6f289cac8587aa9d66898/progress)
retains the original evidence. Source Markdown links point to those immutable
artifacts. Compact benchmark/metric summaries and directly referenced delivery
proof remain local. No acceptance result was changed.

New routine output belongs in ignored `test-results/` or CI artifacts. Keep a
short record of the command, revision, result and limitations here. Preserve
release-critical evidence durably before a CI artifact expires. Do not add
copies of source code, generated specifications or session transcripts.
